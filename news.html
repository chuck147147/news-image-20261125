No. 01 — SAUDI ARABIA · RIYADH

Drone Strikes Riyadh's Tallest Tower

Eleven wounded, Saudi civil defence says, as a Houthi drone reaches the heart of the capital.

A Houthi drone struck the tallest tower in Riyadh on Tuesday, wounding eleven people, according to Saudi civil defence officials — the deepest strike into the kingdom's capital since the war across the Red Sea began its latest escalation.

The Saudi-led coalition condemned the attack, calling it a deliberate strike on a civilian landmark. Air defences were heard over the city shortly before the impact.

There was no immediate claim detailing the launch site. The strike underlines how far the conflict's reach now stretches — from the shipping lanes of Hormuz to the skylines of the Gulf's capitals.
